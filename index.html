Use normally-open momentary buttons. Internal pull-ups are enabled. On a four-leg tactile switch, use the two contact groups that connect only when pressed. No external resistors required.

Start driving

  1. Upload the new ESP32 sketch. Close Arduino Serial Monitor.
  2. Run this folder with Live Server in desktop Chrome or Edge.
  3. Click Connect ESP32, choose the port, then release both buttons.
  4. Press the encoder switch to center, hold the accelerator, and steer.

Braking wins if both buttons are pressed. After a crash, reset, pause or lost connection, release both buttons before driving again. Keyboard acceleration and braking also work.

Includes moving traffic, guardrail collisions, vehicle spin, body damage, debris, sparks, smoke, engine and impact sounds. Severe crashes may show a brief fire effect. This is an arcade simulation, not a real vehicle safety model.
